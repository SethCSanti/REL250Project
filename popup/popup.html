What is this?
Still Small Voice is a gentle browser companion for students and anyone who works at a computer. Hover the gold tab on any page, share how you're feeling, and receive a verse or quote matched to that moment.

How to use
Hover the gold sliver on the right edge of any page to open the sidebar. Choose your emotion and a verse or quote will appear. Hit Another for a different one, or Save to keep it here.
